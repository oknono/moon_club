Meet the founder, or see what's on.

Sayings of the Founder, as recorded by the Familiar

The Founder

President, treasurer, and sole permanent member.

I started the club because the other option was the window.

The window is fine. I have given it a great deal of attention. I know when the light reaches the third floorboard, and which bird comes second. It is a good window. It is not the same as what is on the other side of it.

I don't complain. I make arrangements.

Some guests are nervous at first, and I understand. I go into the other room for a few minutes myself. Then I come back, and we begin.

I listen to horns that take their time. I am fond of old things that stay put and look out over a long distance. The Egyptians understood us, and I have not seen the evidence that anyone since has done better.

I bite when it is called for. It is called for less often than the Familiar says and more often than you would expect.

The Familiar is a good one. It took us a while. We are still working on the door.

House rules

  • Arrive. Be ignored for a while. This is normal.
  • The good chair is taken.
  • Laps are by invitation. The invitation is not spoken.
  • The cake is for the club.

To watch one window for long enough is to see the whole sky.

The Familiar

I keep the domain. He keeps the hours. Between us we keep the flat, which is the part neither of us chose.

The window was his first choice of venue. There was a motion put forward and a vote taken, and I had both votes. He took the loss the way he takes most things: briefly, and then at length, from a distance, with his back to me.

The vote was not a vote.

Added by the other author, who walked across the keyboard.

The club was his idea, and I want that on record. I only supplied the electricity, the cake, and the kettle. I am told the title of Familiar is an honour. I have checked the contract and I think it is more of a posting.

Some terms of attendance. When you arrive he will leave the room. Let him. He goes to the bedroom for about five minutes, and then he comes back as if the first part didn't happen, and sits on you. Don't reach for him before then. Hands are not covered by the club's insurance, and there is no insurance.

This is my first cat. I love him, and he is also, on certain days, a bit of an asshole. Both are accurate, and neither is up for revision.

The wise cat does not chase the light. The wise cat knows who holds it.

Sayings of the Founder, as recorded by the Familiar

Character Sheet

Vampire: The Masquerade. He is the storyteller. This is also his character.

Background

He was not made so much as received. The details of the night are his to keep. There was a bright room, a quiet procedure and a long, patient drive home, and he came out of it the same in every way that matters to him, and different in one way that closed a door for good.

He has seen the outside the way the Ancients see the sun: through glass, from a safe distance, with something close to longing. He does not say what he thinks of the arrangement, and he is not required to. He keeps a haven, a court of one, and a Familiar who is bound to him by a contract nobody has read.

Strangers are met with distance. After five minutes, with the right guest, there is sometimes a lap.

Sayings of the Founder, as recorded by the Familiar

Profile

Seeking. Discreetly. Middle-aged, so you know what you're getting.

About me

Sleeps a lot. Awake when it matters. Likes jazz that isn't trying to be liked. Will bite once, hard, to see what you do about it.

Looking for

Tall. Quiet. A person who answers a question with a look. You glance at your phone while I'm talking and I find it restful.

Not looking for

  • Kittens. Not on offer. Don't ask.
  • “He's just being friendly.”
  • Anyone too eager. I'll warm up. Give me five minutes.
  • Dogs.

Ideal first date

A window. Something on in the background. You, across the room, pretending I'm not the reason you came.

Location: indoors. Distance: not far. Exactly as far as the door.

Tea is the shortest distance between two strangers.

Recordings

The birds are recorded through glass, the theremin is played at a distance, and the pad is mostly a night thing. None of it is released. Recommended listening is anything where the horn goes somewhere and doesn't explain itself, played in the dark.

Heard in the lounge

  • Pharaoh Sanders, with the lights off.
  • Mulatu Astatke and the Ethiopian jazz of the 1970s, mid-afternoon.
  • Alice Coltrane, when the weather turns.
  • Sun Ra, for the long evenings.

In progress

Nothing here is finished, and the Founder would like that understood as a position.

  • Magpie, 6:12am. A field recording, through the glass. Eleven minutes. Still being listened to.
  • Starlings, Gutter. A field recording. The tail is audible if you know to listen for it.
  • Theremin Sketch, Hand Hovering. No contact required. The instrument that suits him best.
  • Pad Study No. 4. Chaos pad, paws only. Not a mistake.
  • Untitled (Door). Recorded the evening of the argument. Do with that what you will.
